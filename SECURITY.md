# Security Policy

RoRo Quantum takes the security of its projects seriously.

## Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability, **please do not open a public issue**.
Instead, use one of the following:

1. **GitHub Security Advisory** (preferred): in the affected repo, go to
   `Security` → `Report a vulnerability` to open a private report.
2. **Email**: security@roroquantum.com

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **72 hours**.
- We will confirm the issue and share a fix/timeline.
- Please keep details private until a fix is released.
- With your permission, we are happy to credit you in the release notes.
